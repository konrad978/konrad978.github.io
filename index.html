Privacy Policy for QR Toolkit

Last updated: August 22, 2026

QR Toolkit is a QR code and barcode scanning and generation application. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the app.

1. Information We Collect

QR Toolkit is designed to work primarily on your device. The app does not require you to create an account.

Depending on the features you use, the app may process the following information:

  • Camera data used to scan QR codes and barcodes.
  • Images selected by you from your device when scanning codes from the gallery or adding images where supported.
  • QR codes and barcodes that you scan or create.
  • Scan history and created-code history stored locally on your device, if you enable history saving.
  • App preferences such as theme, language, vibration, sound and scanner settings.
  • Purchase information processed through Google Play Billing when you purchase QR Toolkit PRO.
  • Advertising-related information processed by Google Mobile Ads where advertisements are displayed.

2. Camera Access

QR Toolkit requests access to your device camera only to scan QR codes and barcodes. Camera images are processed for code recognition and are not uploaded by QR Toolkit to our own servers.

3. Photos and Images

If you choose an image from your device, QR Toolkit uses that image only to perform the action you requested, such as scanning a QR code from the image.

QR Toolkit does not automatically upload your personal photos to our own servers.

4. Local Data

Some app data may be stored locally on your device, including scan history, created QR codes, favorites and application preferences.

You can clear stored history from the application settings. Uninstalling the app may also remove locally stored app data, subject to Android system behavior.

5. Google Play Billing

QR Toolkit offers optional paid features through Google Play. Purchases may include subscriptions and one-time purchases.

Payments are processed by Google Play. QR Toolkit does not receive or store your full payment card details.

Google may process purchase and payment information according to its own privacy policies and terms.

6. Advertising

The free version of QR Toolkit may display advertisements using Google Mobile Ads.

Google and its advertising partners may process information such as advertising identifiers, device information, approximate location or interaction data where permitted by law and according to your consent choices.

Where required, the app may request advertising consent using Google's consent tools.

7. Third-Party Services

QR Toolkit may use services provided by Google, including:

  • Google Play Billing
  • Google Mobile Ads
  • Google User Messaging Platform
  • Google ML Kit
  • Google Play Services

These services may process data according to their own privacy policies.

8. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information.

Data may be processed by third-party service providers only where necessary to provide application functionality, advertising, billing or related services.

9. Children's Privacy

QR Toolkit is a general-purpose utility application. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children directly through an account system.

Advertising and other third-party services may be subject to additional age-related requirements and policies.

10. Security

We take reasonable steps to limit the collection of personal information and to keep app data local where practical.

However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

11. Your Choices

Depending on your device and region, you may be able to:

  • Disable camera permission in Android settings.
  • Clear locally stored scan history.
  • Change advertising privacy choices where available.
  • Cancel subscriptions through Google Play.
  • Uninstall QR Toolkit at any time.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when QR Toolkit changes, when new features are introduced or when legal requirements change.

The updated version will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
